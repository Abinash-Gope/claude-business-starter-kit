# 🔄 3. Follow-up Sequences

## Why Consistent Follow-up Closes Deals

Most sales occur after 5+ follow-up touchpoints. A systematic cadence prevents prospects from falling through the cracks.

---

## 📅 Post-Demo Follow-up Cadence (7 Touchpoints)

| Day | Channel | Content | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Email | Call recap + proposal link | Confirm next steps |
| Day 3 | Email | Relevant case study/insight | Build value |
| Day 5 | LinkedIn | Comment on their post or send soft message | Stay visible |
| Day 7 | Phone / Email | Quick check-in call/email | Answer questions |
| Day 10 | Email | Value drop (free resource/tool) | Re-engage |
| Day 14 | Email | Final proposal reminder | Decision check |
| Day 21 | Email | Soft break-up email | Re-activate |

---

## 📋 Follow-up Best Practices

- [ ] Include personalized notes from demo in every message
- [ ] Share specific case studies matching prospect's niche
- [ ] Use multi-channel outreach (Email + LinkedIn + Call)
- [ ] Set automated reminders in CRM for each touchpoint
