# 📞 1. Sales Call Scripts

## Why Scripted Calls Don't Mean Rigid Calls

Scripts ensure key discovery, value proposition, and closing moments are never missed while allowing for natural conversation flow.

---

## 📞 Discovery Call Script (SPIN Framework)

### Situation Questions
- *"Walk me through your current process for [pain]."*
- *"Who else is involved in this decision?"*

### Problem Questions
- *"What is the biggest challenge with that process?"*

### Implication Questions
- *"If this remained unchanged, what would be the cost over 12 months?"*

### Need-Payoff Questions
- *"If we solved this, what would that mean for your KPIs?"*

---

## 🎯 Demo Call Structure

1. **Set Agenda** (2 min): What we will cover.
2. **Problem Recap** (5 min): Align on core pain points.
3. **Solution Walkthrough** (15 min): Feature-to-benefit mapping.
4. **Q&A** (10 min): Open floor for objections.
5. **Next Steps** (5 min): Proposal or pilot agreement.

---

## 📋 Sales Call Checklist

- [ ] Prepare 3 discovery questions ahead
- [ ] Send agenda 24 hours before call
- [ ] Record notes within 30 minutes of call
- [ ] Follow up with recap email within 2 hours
