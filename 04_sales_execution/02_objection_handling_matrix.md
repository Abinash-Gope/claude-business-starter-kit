# 🛡️ 2. Handle Objections

## Why Objection Handling Wins Trust

Every objection is an opportunity to build confidence. Handling objections with empathy and evidence converts hesitation into commitment.

---

## 🧱 Objection Battlecards

### Price Objections
- **Objection**: *"It's too expensive."*
- **Response**: *"Let's look at the ROI. For every $1 you invest, we deliver $5 in pipeline. Would you prefer to pay $1 and gain $5, or pay $0 and stay where you are?"*

### Time Objections
- **Objection**: *"We don't have time to onboard."*
- **Response**: *"We handle 80% of the onboarding for you. Our average client goes live in 14 days. We'll manage the entire process so your team stays focused."*

### Authority Objections
- **Objection**: *"We need to discuss this with my team."*
- **Response**: *"That's smart. Here is a slide deck your team can review. Let's schedule a follow-up call with them to address any questions."*

### Trust Objections
- **Objection**: *"We've worked with agencies before that didn't deliver."*
- **Response**: *"We understand that risk. That's why we start with a 30-day pilot with clear KPIs. If we don't hit the mark, you don't pay."*

### Need Objections
- **Objection**: *"This isn't a priority right now."*
- **Response**: *"What is your top priority this quarter? If we can show how this solves it, would it move up the list?"*

---

## 📋 Objection Handling Checklist

- [ ] Acknowledge and validate the objection before responding
- [ ] Isolate the objection (confirm it's the real blocker)
- [ ] Use evidence (case study, data, guarantee)
- [ ] Close with a question to re-engage the buyer
