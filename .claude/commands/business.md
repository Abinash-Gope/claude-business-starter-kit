description: Run the Claude Business Starter Kit strategist on your project or question
---

Evaluate the user's project, business idea, or current challenge using the 26 playbooks in the Claude Business Starter Kit.

1. **Assess the context**:
   - Determine which phase the user needs:
     - Phase 1: Strategy, Niche, Competitors, ICP, Offer & Pricing (`01_strategy/`)
     - Phase 2: Website, Landing Page, Content, Ads, Funnel (`02_web_and_marketing/`)
     - Phase 3: Prospect Sourcing, Lead Magnets, Cold Email, Personalization, Proposals (`03_lead_gen_and_outreach/`)
     - Phase 4: Sales Scripts, Objection Handling, Follow-ups, Case Studies (`04_sales_execution/`)
     - Phase 5: CRM Setup, Client Onboarding, Automations, Retention (`05_operations_and_retention/`)
     - Phase 6: Core Metrics, CRO Optimization, Pre-Launch QA Checklists (`06_analytics_and_qa/`)

2. **Read the relevant playbook file(s)** from the skill directory.
3. **Execute actionable output**:
   - Deliver concrete, tailored deliverables (e.g. customized cold email drafts, pricing tier tables, objection battlecards, or landing page wireframes) rather than generic advice.
   - Attach the corresponding checklist so the user can track their execution.
