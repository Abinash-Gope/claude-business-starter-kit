# 🤝 2. Customer Onboarding

## Why Onboarding Sets Up Long-Term Retention

A smooth onboarding experience builds momentum, reduces anxiety, and accelerates time-to-value for new clients.

---

## 🚀 Onboarding Workflow (First 14 Days)

### Day 1: Welcome & Intake
- Send welcome email with login credentials
- Deliver client intake questionnaire
- Schedule Kickoff Call

### Day 3: Kickoff Meeting
- Review goals, metrics, and timeline
- Align on roles and communication channels (Slack/Email)

### Day 7: Strategy Approval
- Present initial strategy/deliverable draft
- Obtain sign-off from client

### Day 14: Execution Check-in
- Review early results and deliver first progress report

---

## 📋 Onboarding Checklist

- [ ] Send automated welcome email immediately after sign-up
- [ ] Share client intake form (access, assets, goals)
- [ ] Host Kickoff Call within 5 days of payment
- [ ] Set up dedicated client channel (Slack/Teams)
- [ ] Deliver first progress update within 14 days
