# 🛡️ 5. Retain Customers

## Why Retention Beats Acquisition

Retaining existing customers costs 5-25x less than acquiring new ones and increases lifetime value (LTV). A proactive retention framework prevents churn and creates expansion opportunities.

---

## 📊 Customer Health Scorecard

| Indicator | Green | Yellow | Red | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Usage Frequency** | Logs in weekly | Logs in bi-weekly | No login in 30 days | Outreach call |
| **NPS Score** | >9 | 7-8 | <7 | Check-in call |
| **Support Tickets** | 0-1/mo | 2-5/mo | >5/mo | Account review |
| **Renewal Timeline** | 60+ days | 30-60 days | <30 days | Renewal proposal |

---

## 📅 Churn Prevention Cadence

### Month 1-3: New Customer Success
- Week 1: Welcome & Introductory Call
- Week 2: First Progress Check
- Week 3: Initial KPI Review
- Week 4: First QBR Light

### Month 4-6: Expansion Opportunities
- Send product upgrade proposal
- Offer complementary services based on usage

### Month 7-12: Renewal & Retention
- Send 60-day renewal notice
- Schedule QBR with success metrics
- Negotiate contract renewal or expansion

---

## 📋 Retention Playbook Checklist

- [ ] Track client health score monthly
- [ ] Schedule quarterly business reviews (QBRs)
- [ ] Set up automated renewal reminders 60/30 days out
- [ ] Create upsell scripts for existing clients
- [ ] Monitor NPS scores and act on feedback
