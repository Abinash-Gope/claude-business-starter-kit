# 📊 1. Track Performance

## Why Metrics-Driven Decisions Scale Faster

Tracking core business KPIs provides objective visibility into health, growth opportunities, and areas needing course correction.

---

## 📈 Core Business Metrics Dashboard

| Metric | Acronym | Definition | Weekly Target | Current | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Customer Acquisition Cost | CAC | Total sales/marketing spend ÷ new customers | $50 or less | $62 | ⚠️ |
| Lifetime Value | LTV | Average revenue per customer × lifespan | $300+ | $280 | ⚠️ |
| Conversion Rate | CR | Leads → paying customers | 5%+ | 4.2% | ⚠️ |
| Monthly Recurring Revenue | MRR | Total recurring revenue this month | $10K+ | $8,500 | ⚠️ |
| Churn Rate | CR | % customers not renewing monthly | <2% | 3.1% | ❌ |

---

## 📋 Performance Tracking Checklist

- [ ] Define 5-7 core KPIs aligned with business goals
- [ ] Set up dashboard (Google Data Studio, Notion, Metabase)
- [ ] Populate with data weekly
- [ ] Compare current vs. prior period
- [ ] Identify top 3 wins and 3 improvements
- [ ] Share insights with stakeholders

---

## 💡 Weekly Metrics Review Format

| Section | Prompt |
|---|---|
| **Wins** | What went well this week? |
| **Misses** | What underperformed? |
| **Hypotheses** | What do we think caused the gap? |
| **Action Items** | What 3 things will we change next week? |
