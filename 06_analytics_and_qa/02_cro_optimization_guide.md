# 📈 2. Improve Conversion Rate

## Why CRO Turns More Visitors into Customers

Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) systematically identifies and removes friction, increasing the percentage of visitors who take your desired action.

---

## 🔍 CRO Audit Framework

### Funnel Friction Audit
- [ ] Heatmap analysis (Hotjar / Microsoft Clarity)
- [ ] Session recording review for drop-off points
- [ ] Form field count and length audit
- [ ] Page load speed check (<3s)
- [ ] Mobile responsiveness review
- [ ] CTA visibility and contrast check

### Messaging Audit
- [ ] Headline clarity vs. visitor intent
- [ ] Value proposition prominence
- [ ] Social proof placement
- [ ] Trust signal presence (guarantees, logos)

---

## 🧪 A/B Testing Roadmap

| Test # | Element | Hypothesis | Duration | Expected Lift |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Headline A vs. B | Outcome-focused copy increases clarity | 2 weeks | +3-5% |
| 2 | CTA Button Color | High-contrast green > default blue | 2 weeks | +1-2% |
| 3 | Form Length 2 fields vs 4 | Fewer fields = higher completion | 2 weeks | +5-10% |
| 4 | Social Proof Position | Testimonial above the fold | 2 weeks | +2-4% |

---

## 📋 CRO Optimization Checklist

- [ ] Audit current funnel conversion rates
- [ ] Identify top 3 friction points
- [ ] Create 5 A/B test hypotheses
- [ ] Run tests for minimum 2 weeks per variant
- [ ] Implement winning variant
- [ ] Repeat cycle monthly
