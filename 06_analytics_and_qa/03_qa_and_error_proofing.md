# ✅ 3. Make No Mistakes

## Why Quality Assurance Prevents Costly Errors

Pre-launch checklists across marketing, sales, technology, and legal workflows prevent missteps that damage reputation and conversion rates.

---

## 🔎 Pre-Launch QA Checklist by Domain

### Marketing & Messaging
- [ ] Website copy reviewed for typos
- [ ] All CTAs link to correct pages
- [ ] Forms submit correctly
- [ ] Social channels consistent

### Technology & Automation
- [ ] Email sequences deploy without errors
- [ ] CRM data fields map correctly
- [ ] Zapier/Make workflows trigger properly
- [ ] Tracking pixels active

### Sales & Outreach
- [ ] Cold email templates personalized correctly
- [ ] Follow-up sequences configured
- [ ] Sales proposal templates ready
- [ ] CRM pipeline stages correct

### Legal & Compliance
- [ ] Terms of service updated
- [ ] Privacy policy compliant
- [ ] GDPR/consent forms in place
- [ ] Contracts reviewed by legal

---

## 🧩 Common Failure Points

| Area | Common Issue | Prevention |
|---|---|---|
| Outreach | Email typo, broken links | Peer review all templates |
| Website | Mobile layout broken | Test on 5 device types |
| Automation | Duplicate Zap errors | Test with test data |

---

## 📋 QA Checklist

- [ ] Run through full customer journey as a user
- [ ] Simulate test purchases/sales calls
- [ ] Conduct peer review of all assets
- [ ] Create a 30-60-90 day post-launch monitoring plan
