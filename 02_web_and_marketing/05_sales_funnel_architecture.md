# 🔻 5. Build a Sales Funnel

## Why Sales Funnels Guide Buyers

A sales funnel maps the customer journey from cold traffic to repeat client. Structuring each stage prevents lead leakage and maximizes customer lifetime value (LTV).

---

## 🗺️ Full-Funnel Architecture Map

```
[ Cold Traffic / Ads / Outreach ]
               │
               ▼
   ┌───────────────────────┐
   │ Top of Funnel (TOFU)  │ ── Lead Magnet / Opt-in Page
   └───────────────────────┘
               │
               ▼
   ┌───────────────────────┐
   │ Middle Funnel (MOFU)  │ ── Thank You / Tripwire / Nurture
   └───────────────────────┘
               │
               ▼
   ┌───────────────────────┐
   │ Bottom Funnel (BOFU)  │ ── Core Offer Proposal / Call
   └───────────────────────┘
               │
               ▼
   ┌───────────────────────┐
   │   Post-Purchase      │ ── Onboarding / Upsell / Retainer
   └───────────────────────┘
```

---

## 📊 Stage Definitions & Metrics

### 1. Top of Funnel (TOFU) - Awareness
- **Goal**: Capture lead contact info
- **Assets**: Free guide, checklist, audit tool, webinar
- **Benchmark**: Opt-in rate >25%

### 2. Middle of Funnel (MOFU) - Consideration
- **Goal**: Build trust and demonstrate authority
- **Assets**: Email nurture series, case studies, product demo
- **Benchmark**: Email open rate >40%, click rate >5%

### 3. Bottom of Funnel (BOFU) - Conversion
- **Goal**: Convert lead into paying customer
- **Assets**: Strategy call, proposal, trial, core offer
- **Benchmark**: Sales call close rate >20%

### 4. Post-Purchase - Retention & Expansion
- **Goal**: Onboard, retain, and upsell
- **Assets**: Kickoff call, QBRs, upsell packages
- **Benchmark**: Monthly churn <2%, Net Retention >110%

## 📋 Sales Funnel Checklist

- [ ] Define entry points for cold traffic
- [ ] Create high-converting lead magnet
- [ ] Build Opt-in Page and Thank You Page
- [ ] Set up 5-email nurture sequence
- [ ] Design BOFU call booking or checkout page
- [ ] Connect CRM pipeline tracking

---

## 💡 Funnel Optimization Checklist

- [ ] Audit drop-off rates at every stage weekly
- [ ] Test lead magnet topics against ICP pain
- [ ] Add retargeting ads for drop-off visitors
