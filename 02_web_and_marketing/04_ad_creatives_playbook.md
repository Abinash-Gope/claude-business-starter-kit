# 🎨 4. Design Ad Creatives

## Why High-Converting Ad Creatives Drive Scalable Growth

Ad creatives are the primary lever for paid customer acquisition. Combining compelling copy, striking visual hooks, and strong CTAs lowers Customer Acquisition Costs (CAC).

---

## 📐 Ad Creative Structure & Copy Frameworks

### 1. Hook (First 3 Seconds)
- **Visual Hook**: Eye-catching image/motion
- **Copy Hook**: "Attention [ICP]", "Stop scrolling if...", "How to [Outcome]"

### 2. Pain / Agitation
- Highlight a specific problem your audience faces.

### 3. Solution / Offer
- Introduce your product/service as the bridge from pain to outcome.

### 4. Call to Action (CTA)
- Clear, friction-free instruction: "Claim Free Trial", "Book Strategy Call".

---

## ✍️ Copy Frameworks

### Framework A: PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solve)
- **Problem**: *"Tired of cold emails ending up in spam?"*
- **Agitate**: *"Every bounced email costs you potential revenue and damages domain reputation."*
- **Solve**: *"Our AI warm-up tool guarantees 98% inbox deliverability in 14 days."*

### Framework B: Before-After-Bridge (BAB)
- **Before**: *"Struggling to generate predictable pipeline?"*
- **After**: *"Imagine waking up to 5 qualified sales calls every morning."*
- **Bridge**: *"Here is the exact cold outreach template we used to achieve this."*

---

## 🎨 Creative Formats & Specs

| Format | Specs | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Single Image | 1080×1080 or 1200×628 | Quick value prop, testimonials |
| Carousel | 1080×1080 (3-5 cards) | Process breakdown, features |
| Video / Reel | 9:16 (1080×1920) | UGC, tutorials, video hooks |
| Motion Graphics | 1:1 or 9:16 | Feature highlights |

---

## 📋 Ad Creatives Checklist

- [ ] Define target ICP and primary pain point
- [ ] Draft 3 hook variations per ad concept
- [ ] Choose ad format (Image, Carousel, Video)
- [ ] Design visual assets with clear branding
- [ ] Add bold, scannable text overlays
- [ ] Include clear CTA button/link
- [ ] A/B test creatives weekly

---

## 📊 Performance Indicators

- **CTR (Click-Through Rate)**: >1.5% target
- **CPC (Cost Per Click)**: Industry benchmark dependent
- **CPA (Cost Per Acquisition)**: Target CAC alignment
- **ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)**: >3x target
