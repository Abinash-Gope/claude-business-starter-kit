# 🛬 2. Build a Landing Page

## Why Landing Pages Convert

A dedicated landing page removes distractions and guides visitors toward a single conversion action. High-converting pages follow a proven layout and copy formula.

---

## 🧱 High-Converting Landing Page Structure

### 1. Hero Section
- **Headline**: Clear value proposition (outcome + timeframe)
- **Subheadline**: Supporting benefit + pain removal
- **CTA Button**: Primary action (e.g., "Get Free Audit")
- **Visual**: Product image, mockup, or video

### 2. Social Proof
- **Logos**: Recognizable clients
- **Testimonials**: Short, specific quotes with metrics
- **Trust Badges**: Guarantees, certifications, media mentions

### 3. Benefits & Features
- **Problem-Agitate-Solution (PAS)** structure
- **Bullet Points**: 3-5 key benefits with outcome focus
- **Visuals**: Screenshots or diagrams

### 4. Offer Details
- **What's Included**: Break down deliverables
- **Price Anchor**: Show value vs. price
- **Bonus Stack**: Add perceived value

### 5. FAQ Section
Anticipate and answer top 5 objections.

### 6. Final CTA Section
Repeat headline + CTA below the fold with minimal distraction.

---

## ✍️ Copy Frameworks

**PAS Formula**:
- **Problem**: Identify pain
- **Agitate**: Magnify consequences
- **Solution**: Present your offer

**AIDA Formula**:
- **Attention**: Hook
- **Interest**: Benefits
- **Desire**: Visualization of outcome
- **Action**: CTA

---

## 📋 Landing Page Build Checklist

- [ ] One clear CTA per page
- [ ] Headline emphasizes outcome
- [ ] Social proof above the fold
- [ ] Scannable format (short paragraphs, bullets)
- [ ] Mobile responsive design
- [ ] Fast load speed (<3s)
- [ ] Form fields minimized (name + email only)

---

## 📊 Metrics to Track

| Metric | Benchmark |
|---|---|
| Conversion Rate | 5-15% for cold traffic |
| Bounce Rate | <50% |
| Time on Page | >60 seconds |
| Form Completion Rate | >60% |

## 💡 Testing Roadmap

- **CTA Buttons**: Color, copy, placement
