# 📅 3. Marketing Content

## Why Strategic Content Drives Inbound Demand

Content builds trust, educates prospects, and positions you as an industry authority. A repeatable calendar keeps messaging consistent across channels.

---

## 🗓️ 30-Day Content Calendar Framework

### Content Pillars
1. **Educational**: How-tos, tutorials, industry insights
2. **Social Proof**: Case studies, client wins, testimonials
3. **Thought Leadership**: Controversial takes, trends, lessons
4. **Offer Focused**: Product features, promos, CTAs

---

## 📅 Monthly Content Template

| Day | Platform | Format | Content Pillar | Hook/Topic | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | LinkedIn | Text + Image | Educational | "3 mistake B2B founders make with..." | Read blog post |
| Tue | Twitter/X | Thread | Thought Leadership | "How we scaled to $50K MRR in 6 months" | Retweet + Follow |
| Wed | YouTube | Video | Educational | "Step-by-step guide to CRM setup" | Subscribe |
| Thu | LinkedIn | Carousel | Social Proof | "How Client X got 50 leads in 30 days" | Book call |
| Fri | Newsletter | Email | Offer | "Weekly strategy teardown + special offer" | Click link |

---

## 🪝 Hook Formulas

- **Mistake Hook**: *"Why 90% of founders fail at [Task] (and how to avoid it)"*
- **Numbers Hook**: *"How we achieved [Outcome] in [Timeframe] using 3 steps"*
- **Contrarian Hook**: *"Stop doing [Common Practice]. Do this instead."*
- **Question Hook**: *"Struggling with [Pain]? Here is the exact fix."*

---

## 📋 Marketing Content Checklist

- [ ] Define 3-4 core content pillars
- [ ] Select 1-2 primary channels (e.g., LinkedIn + Email)
- [ ] Create 30-day content calendar
- [ ] Batch content creation (write 1x/week)
- [ ] Monitor top-performing content and repurpose
- [ ] Include clear CTAs on every post

---
