# 🎯 4. Personalize Outreach

## Why Generic Outreach Gets Ignored

Personalized outreach increases reply rates because it shows genuine attention instead of copy-paste spam.

---

## 🔹 First-Touch Personalization Framework

### Variables to Pull In
- `{First Name}`: Prospect's first name
- `{Company Name}`: Their company
- `{Trigger Event}`: Recent funding round, hire, product launch
- `{Mutual Connection}`: Shared LinkedIn connection
- `{Industry Pain}`: Specific pain common to their sector

### Hook Template
```
Hi {First Name}, noticed {Company Name} just launched {Product/Feature}. Congrats! We helped {Similar Company} achieve [Outcome] by solving {Pain}. Would love to share 1 idea that could help you too.
```

---

## 📋 Personalization Checklist

- [ ] Research prospect's LinkedIn for recent activity
- [ ] Reference mutual connection or company news
- [ ] Use custom icebreaker per recipient
- [ ] Match tone to recipient's personality/culture
- [ ] Track personalized vs. non-personalized reply rates
