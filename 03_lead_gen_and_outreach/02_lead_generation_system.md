# 🚀 2. Generate Leads

## Why a Systematized Lead Machine Drives Predictable Growth

Instead of sporadic prospecting, a repeatable lead generation system provides a consistent flow of qualified leads into your pipeline.

---

## 🧩 Lead Generation Channels Matrix

| Channel | Type | Effort | Lead Quality | Avg. CPL |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn Ads | Paid | Medium | High | $5 - $15 |
| Cold Email | Outbound | Low | Medium-High | $2 - $8 |
| SEO Blog | Organic | High | High | $0 (long-term) |
| Referrals | Organic | Low | Very High | $0 |
| Events/Webinars | Event | High | Medium | $20 - $50 |

---

## 🧲 Inbound Lead Magnet Blueprint

1. **Deliverable**: Solve one small, urgent pain in 5 minutes or less.
2. **Format**: Checklist, Calculator, Audit, Guide, Template.
3. **Promotion**: Promote via website, LinkedIn posts, newsletter footer.

> Example: "*Free CRM ROI Calculator for B2B Marketing Teams*"

---

## 📋 Lead Generation Checklist

- [ ] Choose 2-3 primary lead sources
- [ ] Create one high-value lead magnet
- [ ] Build a landing page with opt-in form
- [ ] Route leads directly to CRM
- [ ] Set up weekly lead volume targets
- [ ] Track source-by-source conversion rates
