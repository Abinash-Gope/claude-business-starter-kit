# 🔍 1. Find Potential Customers

## Why Targeted Sourcing Maximizes Response Rates

Building a verified, hyper-targeted prospect list ensures your cold outreach reaches real decision-makers who need your solution.

---

## 🎯 Sourcing Channels & Tools

### 1. LinkedIn Sales Navigator
- **Filters**: Title, Industry, Company Headcount, Geography, Keywords
- **Boolean Search**: `("VP Marketing" OR "Head of Marketing") AND "B2B"`

### 2. Apollo.io / Hunter.io
- Access company tech stacks, direct dials, and verified emails.

### 3. Community Sourcing
- Slack communities, Reddit, Slack/Discord industry groups, events.

---

## 📋 Prospect Sourcing Checklist

- [ ] Define target ICP criteria (Industry, Title, Company Size)
- [ ] Build Boolean search strings
- [ ] Source 100-500 targeted prospects weekly
- [ ] Verify email addresses using ZeroBounce or NeverBounce
- [ ] Exclude current clients and opted-out leads
- [ ] Import leads into outreach CRM with custom fields

---

## 🛠️ List Cleaning SOP

```
Raw List -> Email Verification -> Remove Bounced/Invalid -> Deduplicate -> Ready for Outreach
```

> **Target Quality**: >95% email validity rate before sending to protect domain reputation.
