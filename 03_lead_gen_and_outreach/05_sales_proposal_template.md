# 📄 5. Sales Proposals

## Why Professional Proposals Win Deals

A structured proposal removes ambiguity, justifies pricing, and builds confidence in your process and timeline.

---

## 🏗️ Proposal Template Structure

### 1. Executive Summary
Concise recap of the prospect's challenge and our intended impact.

### 2. Scope of Work
Break down deliverables week-by-week with responsible parties.

### 3. Key Deliverables Table

| Task | Week | Owner | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery Call | Week 1 | Consultant | Defined goals |
| Onboarding | Week 2 | Project Manager | Kickoff complete |
| Execution | Weeks 3-8 | Consultant | Campaign live |

### 4. Pricing Matrix

| Package | Core Service + Support | Total |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | [X] Hours + Tools | $XXX |
| Premium | [X] Hours + Extras | $XXX |

### 5. Terms & Conditions
Payment schedule (50% upfront, 50% mid-project), cancellation policy, confidentiality.

### 6. Acceptance Block
Signature line, date, printed name.

---

## 📋 Proposal Delivery Checklist

- [ ] Send follow-up call scheduling link immediately after proposal
- [ ] Allow 3 business days for decision-making
- [ ] Track open rates via DocuSign or PandaDoc
- [ ] Schedule debrief if declined for feedback loop
