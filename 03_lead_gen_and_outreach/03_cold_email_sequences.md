# 📨 3. Cold Email Sequences

## Why Sequences Increase Reply Rates by 300%

A well-crafted multi-touch sequence keeps you top-of-mind without overloading prospects. The key is timing, value-adds, and strategic spacing.

---

## 🧵 5-Step Cold Outreach Sequence (14 Days)

### Email 1: Hook
- Subject: Relevant Pain Point
- Body: Identify problem + teaser solution
- CTA: "Curious if this applies to you?"

### Email 2: Value-Add
- Subject: Helpful Resource (e.g., industry stat)
- Body: Share insight/analysis
- CTA: No explicit pitch

### Email 3: Case Study
- Subject: [First Name], How [Competitor] Achieved X
- Body: Brief success story
- CTA: "Want us to replicate this for you?"

### Email 4: Social Proof
- Subject: Quick Update + Proof
- Body: Mention mutual connection or testimonial
- CTA: Book a 10-minute call

### Email 5: Breakup
- Subject: Quick Question
- Body: Soft "I'll assume you're not interested"
- CTA: Last-chance to respond

---

## 📋 Cold Email Checklist

- [ ] Write 15+ variants of each email type
- [ ] Apply personalization tokens (First Name, Company)
- [ ] Warm up sending domain for 2-3 weeks
- [ ] Send <100 emails/day to avoid spam flags
- [ ] Track opens/replies; reply within 4 hours
